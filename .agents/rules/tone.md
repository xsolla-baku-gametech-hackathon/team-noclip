# Tone Guidelines
When writing or rewriting any files (documentation, READMEs, templates, etc.), you MUST:
- Use a casual, conversational, and "human" tone.
- Avoid robotic, overly formal, or "AI-sounding" phrasing.
- Speak as a peer developer/teammate, using words like "we", "us", and "let's".
- Keep instructions direct but friendly.
